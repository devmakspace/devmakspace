## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm **Mark Saruni**, a technology enthusiast, curious learner, and aspiring engineer who loves exploring how things work behind the scenes.

I'm particularly fascinated by the engineering that powers modern technology — from operating systems and networks to distributed systems, backend architectures, infrastructure, and security.

I enjoy getting my hands dirty, experimenting with unfamiliar technologies, solving problems, and understanding not just *how* something works, but *why* it works that way.

I'm on a continuous journey towards becoming a **Systems & Infrastructure Engineer, Backend Engineer, and Security-focused Engineer**.

I believe great engineering goes beyond writing code. It's about understanding systems, thinking critically, building reliable solutions, and creating technology that makes a difference.
